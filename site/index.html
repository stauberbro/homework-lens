The menu

Four tools, one sidebar.

Every part of school work has a spot in the menu. Pick the one you need and start.

Teach me

Missed a class? Type the topic or what the teacher talked about, and get a lesson one step at a time.

  • Add a photo of the board, your notebook or the textbook page.
  • A quick check after every step, so you know it stuck.
  • Tap “I don't get it” and the step is explained another way.
  • Ends with what you learned, your score, and a quiz on the lesson.
  • Ask anything about the lesson at any point.

Homework help

Take a photo of the worksheet, drop in a file, or paste a screenshot with Ctrl+V. Then pick what you need.

  • Explain it: what is this about?
  • Walk me through: the solution, step by step.
  • Check my work: finds your mistakes.
  • Quiz me: practice questions on the same thing.
  • Point at one part (“I don't get question 4”) and ask follow-ups.

Exam prep 9:52

Type the topics, paste your notes, or add up to 4 photos of a worksheet, old test or study guide.

  • Pick Easy, Medium or Hard.
  • Practice questions with the answers hidden until you check, and more when you want them.
  • A 10-minute quiz: new questions with drawings, or the ones from your photos.
  • Review every answer, then quiz yourself on your mistakes or have them explained.

Answers NOW

No lesson, no steps. Type or snap the question and get the answer first, with one line on why.

  • For checking yourself fast, the night before it's due.
  • Works from a typed question or a photo.
What is 15% of 80? 12 15% means 15 out of 100, so 0.15 × 80 = 12.
The 10-minute quiz

Test day, before test day.

Keep scrolling. This is how a quiz runs.

  1. 10 questions, 10 minutes

    The clock starts when all 10 are ready. Jump to any question, or press A, B, C or D to answer.

  2. The clock comes with you

    Open another tool and the timer keeps running in the sidebar, right next to Exam prep.

  3. A next quiz that fits

    See what you got right and why. Your next quiz gets easier or harder based on your score.
